(06) • FAQ
What is RAASLEELA? +

RAASLEELA is a collective Dandiya celebration bringing together multiple foundations and their Dandiya events under one unified celebration in Silchar.

When is RAASLEELA happening? +

RAASLEELA will take place on 13th October 2026.

Where is RAASLEELA being held? +

The event will be held at Assam Palace, Silchar.

Who is presenting RAASLEELA? +

RAASLEELA is presented by Karma Social Foundation.

Who are the organizers of RAASLEELA? +

RAASLEELA is collectively organized through Swarang Foundation’s Rang Raas – 1st Edition, SocioHero Foundation & Empower Express’s Navrass – Chapter 3, Aarambh Foundation’s Raas-E-Raat – Chapter 2, and Aidora Foundation’s Shubhaarambh.

Is Rang Raas still part of the event? +

Yes. Rang Raas – 1st Edition is Swarang Foundation’s contribution to the collective RAASLEELA celebration.

Are registrations open? +

Yes. Registrations are currently open.

Where can I get my tickets? +

Tickets can be accessed through the existing ticket link in the website/bio. Use the existing CTA rather than creating a new ticketing system.

Is RAASLEELA one event or multiple separate events? +

RAASLEELA is one collective Dandiya celebration bringing together the participating foundations and their respective event identities.

What should I expect at RAASLEELA? +

A large-scale Dandiya celebration bringing together music, dance, festive energy and the combined efforts of multiple organizations.
